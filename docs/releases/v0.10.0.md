Copy the text from anywhere on the screen — an idea from my wife, who asked whether you could just pick an area and get its text. You can now.

## New

**Copy text from the screen (`Ctrl+Alt+T`).** The screenshot overlay, for text: drag an area, click a window, or press `Enter` for the whole screen, and the text in it goes straight to the clipboard. A dialog you cannot select from, a line in a video, part of a picture open in another program — no screenshot to open, no menu. Like `Ctrl+C` `C`, the text is also logged as a plain entry in today's journal, so `Ctrl+K` finds it again later; the picture itself is not kept. The notice says how many words were copied and opens the entry. It is also in the tray menu as *Copy text from screen*.

**Sharper reading of small text.** Small areas and pictures are now read at three times their size (larger ones at twice, as before), which mostly ends the recogniser's habit of mixing up `1`, `l` and `I`, or `0` and `O`, in small screen fonts.

## Upgrading

Replace `omniaware.exe`, or run `scripts\install.ps1` again. `config.toml` gains `text = "ctrl+alt+KeyT"` under `[hotkeys]` on first start; set it to `""` to turn the shortcut off. As always, `F1` lists everything.
